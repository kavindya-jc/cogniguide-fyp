Pip, the coach

idle · welcome, home, empty
thinking · typing, loading
happy · growth sentence
sleepy · busy, log out

One Pip per screen, always with words. Not used: celebrate, oops, sad (no rewards, no "wrong" feeling). Stops moving with reduced motion.

Motion

  • Rise in: content fades up 14px, 0.55s, staggered 50ms per item.
  • Pop: new chat bubbles scale from 0.94, 0.35s with a small spring.
  • Typing: three teal dots bounce, 1.2s loop. Pip switches to thinking.
  • Chart: line draws in 1.4s, then points pop one by one.
  • Sheet: slides up 0.45s over a fading backdrop.
  • Press: buttons, chips and keys scale to 0.97 (keys 0.92).
  • Fix messages: drop in gently, no shaking.
  • All motion stops under the phone's reduce-motion setting.

Flutter: AnimatedSlide + AnimatedOpacity, or flutter_animate; Curves.easeOutCubic.
